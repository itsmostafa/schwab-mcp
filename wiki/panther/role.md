# The Role — AI Engineer, SOC Automation

## What They Need

From the job description, three pillars:

**Technical Expertise**
- Embeddings, vector databases, RAG, agentic engineering frameworks
- Production-grade software development (not just prototypes)
- ML/AI agent architectures and feedback loops

**Domain Knowledge**
- SOC workflows, alert triage, threat analysis (hands-on, not just theoretical)
- XDR platforms and security automation challenges
- Passion for solving SOC operational problems through intelligent automation

**Mindset**
- Customer-obsessed: engages directly with customers to listen, learn, build
- Startup mentality: comfortable with ambiguity, excited to build novel solutions
- Product vision: translate technical capabilities into SOC team outcomes

## What You'll Do

- Build/enhance 4+ autonomous AI agents (alert triage, chat, detection codegen, text-to-search)
- Create feedback loops between agents + Panther's ingestion pipeline
- Develop collective intelligence framework (agents learn from analyst observations)
- Partner with customers to understand SOC workflows and validate agent effectiveness
- Drive measurable impact: reduce triage time, enable exponential alert coverage

## Mostafa's Differentiators

Per Darwayne:
- **Go experience** — gives edge over other candidates
- **Python experience** — essential for AI/ML tooling and Panther's detection rules
- **Security background** — rare combination; most AI engineers don't have this
- **AI engineering experience** — directly relevant to the 4 agent areas

## What to Emphasize in Every Round

1. You've worked across the full stack Panther cares about: security + AI + systems
2. You're comfortable owning things end-to-end (startup mentality)
3. You've engaged with end users / customers, not just built in isolation
4. You understand the pain of the problem from a security practitioner's perspective
5. You can build production-grade systems, not just experiments

## Red Flags to Avoid Projecting

- Being purely theoretical about AI (they want builders)
- Needing fully-spec'd requirements before starting
- Siloed experience (only AI or only security, not both)
- Lack of customer empathy (this is explicitly called out)
