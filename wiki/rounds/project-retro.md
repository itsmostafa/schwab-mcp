# Project Retrospective Round

## What They're Looking For

A structured walk-through of a real project you built or led. They want to understand:
- How you approach ambiguous problems
- How you made key architectural/product decisions
- What you learned from failures or pivots
- Your ability to communicate technical work clearly
- Your ownership mindset

## Choosing a Project

Pick a project that:
- Has real impact you can quantify
- Involved meaningful technical decisions (not just execution)
- Has a good failure/learning story (shows self-awareness)
- Is directly relevant to Panther's domain (AI, security, agents, data pipelines)

**Best candidates** (given Mostafa's background):
- Any AI agent or LLM-powered system you built
- Security tooling, detection systems, or SOC-adjacent work
- Data pipeline or real-time processing system
- Something with a clear before/after improvement

## Story Structure (STAR + Decision Layer)

**Situation**: Set context. Company, team, what existed before.

**Task**: What problem were you solving? Why did it matter?

**Architecture/Decisions**: This is the key layer. Walk through 2-3 significant technical decisions:
- What were the options?
- What did you choose?
- Why?
- What trade-offs did you accept?

**Action**: What did you actually build/do?

**Result**: Quantified impact. "Reduced triage time by X%", "handled Nx more alerts with same headcount", etc.

**Retrospective**: What would you do differently? What did you learn? This is what makes it a "retro" not just a project summary.

## Common Questions

- "Walk me through a project you're proud of."
- "Tell me about a time you had to make a technical decision with incomplete information."
- "Describe a project that didn't go as planned. What happened?"
- "What's the most complex system you've designed from scratch?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to convince your team to adopt a different approach."
