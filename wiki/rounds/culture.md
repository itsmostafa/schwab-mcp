# Culture Round

## Panther's 3 Core Values

1. **Create Customer Love** — customer-obsessed, engage directly with customers, build what solves real problems
2. **Be an Owner** — take initiative, don't wait to be told, care about outcomes not just tasks
3. **Take Care of the Team** — collaborative, diverse perspectives, building a rewarding place to work

## What to Emphasize

From the job description, they specifically call out:
- **Customer-obsessed**: thrives on engaging directly with customers
- **Startup mentality**: comfortable with ambiguity, excited by novel solutions
- **Product vision**: translate technical capabilities into meaningful outcomes for SOC teams

## Likely Questions (Behavioral)

Map your answers to the 3 values above.

**Create Customer Love**
- "Tell me about a time you built something that users actually loved."
- "How have you engaged with customers/end users to validate what you're building?"
- "Describe a time you changed direction based on customer feedback."

**Be an Owner**
- "Tell me about a time you took initiative without being asked."
- "Describe a project where you owned the outcome end-to-end."
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision and what you did."

**Take Care of the Team**
- "How do you handle disagreements with teammates?"
- "Tell me about a time you helped a teammate through a difficult problem."
- "How do you give and receive feedback?"

## General Culture Signals to Project

- Comfort with ambiguity: "I don't need a fully spec'd ticket to start adding value"
- Remote-first maturity: async communication, documentation, proactive updates
- Security practitioner mindset: the founders lived the pain they're solving — show you have too
- Enthusiasm for the problem: genuine interest in SOC automation is a differentiator here
