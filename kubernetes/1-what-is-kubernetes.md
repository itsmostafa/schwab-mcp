# What is Kubernetes

- Kubernetes originated froom Google
- Donated to CNCF in 2014 (Open Source)
- Written in Go/Golang
- Kubernetes was built out of to other projects called **Borg** and **Omega**
- The name **Kubernetes** comes from the greek word for "Helmsman" which is the person who steers a ship
- **K8s** is short for *Kubernetes*

## Kubernetes: What and Why
- Containers bring scalability challenges that Kubernetes can solve
- Data centers can be viewed as a computer
- Kubernetes can manage containers based on instructions in a manifest
- Kubernetes is strongly positioned
- Very cloud platform agnostic
