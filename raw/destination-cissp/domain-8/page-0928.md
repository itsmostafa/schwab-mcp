 Security should be considered at every phase of SDLC/SLC.
 Risk analysis and threat modeling are very important components of
the early phases of SDLC/SLC.
 Testing should include static, dynamic, and fuzz (a form of dynamic
testing) tests.
 Certification and accreditation should be performed prior to
release/deployment/implementation.
