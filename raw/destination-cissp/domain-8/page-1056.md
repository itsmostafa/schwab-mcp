The CISSP exam—What to expect
 The majority of questions will be in a multiple-choice
format—four possible answers, and you must select
one answer.
 You may see drag-and-drop questions and these
questions require matching a list of terms or steps
in a process with their corresponding definition or
description. Partial scores are not awarded for these
types of questions. You must get all items correct to
score the points associated with a particular drag-
and-drop question.
 You will likely see questions on material you never
studied or heard about. Be prepared, and don’t let it
psyche you out. Use our guidance and strategies for
selecting the best possible answer, even if you don’t
fully understand the question.
 The Computer Adaptive Test (CAT) algorithm is good
at finding weak topic areas. If any are identified, you
will likely see multiple questions related to the
topic(s). Be prepared and don’t let this psyche you
out.
