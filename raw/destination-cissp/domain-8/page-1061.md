 Eat normally on exam day, but nothing too heavy, and
stay hydrated. Healthier food and good hydration
will allow your body and mind to function optimally
during the exam.
 Just like an athlete warms up prior to a competition,
warm up for your exam with ten to fifteen practice
questions to engage your mind and get in “test
mode.”
 Before taking your exam, look yourself in the mirror
and give yourself a positive pep talk—remind
yourself of the time and effort you’ve put into
preparing, the people who believe and support you,
and why you’re going to pass the exam. You’ve got
this!
