Understand when security should be considered as
part of the SDLC
Many concepts covered in Domain 8 have already been
covered in one manner or another in other domains.
Security needs to be included as an integral part of the
software development life cycle (SDLC) as well as the system
life cycle (SLC). As the name suggests, the SDLC focuses on
the development of an application; the SLC picks up at the
point where an application is put into production and
focuses on use and testing, changes, decommissioning, and
disposal.
