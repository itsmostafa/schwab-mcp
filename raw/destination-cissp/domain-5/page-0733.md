Figure 5-14: RBAC Operation
Implementing Full-RBAC across an entire organization is often very difficult
and counterproductive. One of the main reasons to implement RBAC is to
reduce access administration burden. When Full-RBAC is implemented
across an entire organization for every system, it is common to end up with
more roles than employees. Hence, Full-RBAC may increase the access
administration burden.
This is one of the reasons most organizations implement Limited or Hybrid-
RBAC, depicted in Figure 5-15.
