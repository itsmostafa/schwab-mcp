Policy enforcement
point (PEP)
PEPs are parts of an application that receive authorization requests
for protected systems and data. They function as gatekeepers and
send the requests to the PDP where they are evaluated. When the
PDP sends back the decision, the PEP enforces it by either granting
or denying access. They are placed throughout an application’s
access points.
Policy decision point
(PDP)
PDPs make decisions on the authorization requests that have been
sent to them by PEPs. They make decisions based upon pre-defined
rules and are generally centralized.
