Fundamentally, the above point to the use of the RMC, or
Reference Monitor Concept, where some type of rules-
based decision-making tool is placed in between subjects
and objects to mediate access, and all activity is logged and
monitored for the sake of accountability and assurance. Any
implementation of the RMC is known as a security kernel.
