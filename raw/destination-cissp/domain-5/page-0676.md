The components of Access Control Services are depicted in
Figure 5-2 and explained in detail in the following section.
These are Identification, Authentication, Authorization and
Accountability. Sometimes, we refer to these last three
processes as AAA.
Figure 5-2: Access Controls Services Components
5.2.2 Identification
CORE CONCEPTS
 Identification is the component of Access Control Services that
uniquely asserts a user’s identity or a process to a system.
 Identification guidelines include: unique identifiers, nondescriptive of
job/role, secure issuance.
