Smart/Memory Cards
Understand the differences between smart and
memory cards
Both smart and memory cards consist of an authentication
factor by ownership, as you have a smart or memory card in
your possession. Both cards store information about the
card holder. Smart and memory cards are summarized in
Table 5-6.
