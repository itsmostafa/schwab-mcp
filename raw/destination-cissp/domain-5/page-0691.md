Vascular
pattern
Vascular pattern scanners examine
veins in a hand and are often used in
testing centers, like the ones where
CISSP and other exams are
administered. The scan allows
biometric information of the exam
candidate to be captured prior to
the exam, and if a break or visit to
the restroom is needed, the hand
can be rescanned and the results
compared to the original. This
ensures that the exam candidate
remains the test taker, and not
somebody else.
Facial
Facial scanners examine an
individual’s facial features and
pattern.
Iris
Iris scanners examine the colored
ring around an individual’s eye.
