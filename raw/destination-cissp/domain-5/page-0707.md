What is session hijacking?
Session management is very important, because without it a major risk exists:
session hijacking (shown in Figure 5-8). In other words, through simple
carelessness or sophisticated technical means, somebody other than a
legitimate user could gain access to a session and use it for malicious
purposes. The preventive measure for session hijacking is session
termination, which is an important component of session management.
