5.5.2 Identity Life Cycle
CORE CONCEPTS
 Identity Life Cycle is composed of three parts: provisioning, review,
revocation.
 Provisioning = upon hire of new employee and when employee changes
roles
 Review (also known as user access review) = should take place as often
as necessary, and more frequently for higher privilege accounts
 Revocation = upon voluntary or involuntary termination
