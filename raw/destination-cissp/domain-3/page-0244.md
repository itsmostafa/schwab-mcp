are rule-based, meaning specific rules dictate how security operates. People
sometimes argue that lattice-based models also provide rules and ask why
they’re not considered rule-based. Lattice-based models do include rules, but
those rules are confined to layers within the model; hence, the term lattice-
based is more applicable. Table 3-3 provides a summary of the various
