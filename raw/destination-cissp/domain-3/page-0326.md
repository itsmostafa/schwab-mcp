Protection and Privacy of Data in the Cloud
What should be a primary concern of an organization considering a move to
the cloud?
Regardless of the cloud service or deployment model utilized, organizations should take every
precaution to ensure that proprietary, personal, and other private information remain protected. In
addition to implementing strong access controls, strong encryption practices should be used when and
where necessary to properly secure this data. This is especially true when an organization makes the
initial decision to move from legacy, on-premises infrastructure to that of a cloud provider. In cases like
this, best practices indicate that data should be encrypted and secured locally and then migrated to the
cloud.
