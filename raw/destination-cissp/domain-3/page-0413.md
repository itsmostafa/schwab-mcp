To share the symmetric key securely with Bob, Alice knows
that she can encrypt it with Bob’s public key and send this
to Bob. Because Bob keeps his private key secure and only
known to himself, he is the only person who can decrypt the
symmetric key sent by Alice. Once decrypted, Bob will then
have the same session key, which will allow him to quickly
decrypt and read Alice’s very large message.
Though simple, this is a great example of hybrid
cryptography—a combination of symmetric and
asymmetric cryptography.
