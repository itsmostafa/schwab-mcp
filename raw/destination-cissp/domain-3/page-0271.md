EAL level definitions and order
Despite the table above illustrating multiple EAL levels, EAL7 is not
necessarily the best for the sake of a vendor marketing and selling its product.
In fact, most organizations will not purchase a product that is rated above
EAL4. Operating systems are typically at EAL3 and firewalls at EAL4.
