3.2.4 Rule-based Models
CORE CONCEPTS
 Information flow models track the flow of information and can help
uncover covert channels.
 Covert channels are unintentional communications paths; two types
exist: storage and timing.
 Clark–Wilson is an integrity-focused rule-based model that includes
three goals and three rules.
 Brewer–Nash—the “Chinese Wall” model—is designed to prevent
conflicts of interest.
