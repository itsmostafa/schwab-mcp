Figure 3-19: Defense in Depth
How many layers of defense does a company use to store and protect research and development
information? Undoubtedly there’s a fence around the building that could be serving as the first layer of
defense. In addition, there might be CCTV cameras, and the fence could even be electric. The
combination of these controls is preventive, detective, and corrective, thus constituting a complete
control. After the fence, guards might be patrolling the area regularly. Next is the perimeter of the
building. Similar to the fence, a combination of preventive, detective, and corrective controls exist.
