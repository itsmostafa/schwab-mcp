Repeating Patterns Must Be Avoided
As already mentioned, several times, one of the most
important things with any cryptography implementation is
the avoidance of patterns. Patterns can severely weaken
cryptography, and numerous failures point to key
management issues that lead to the emergence of
numerous patterns.
