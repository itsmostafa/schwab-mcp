3.4.2 Processors (CPUs)
CORE CONCEPTS
 A central processing unit (CPU) is the brain of a computer; it processes
all of the instructions and ultimately solves problems.
 CPU processing involves an ongoing, four-step process: Fetch, Decode,
Execute, and Store.
 A CPU operates in one of two states: the supervisor state or the problem
state.
A CPU is the component within a computer that processes all the instructions.
