3.7.1 Cryptanalysis
CORE CONCEPTS
 Cryptanalysis is a multidisciplinary science.
 Two primary types of cryptanalysis attacks exist: cryptanalytic attacks
and cryptographic attacks.
 The main purpose of cryptanalysis is to deduce or figure out the key
(since in cryptography, everything is known except for the key).
Cryptanalysis is the science of:
