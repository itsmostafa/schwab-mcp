X.509—Digital Certificate Standard
What is the digital certifi cate standard?
For purposes of interoperability and ease of understanding by browsers, each
CA needs to create certificates that are consistent in their formats. As such, all
certificate authorities conform to the X.509 certificate standard, which
contains fields like certificate version, serial number, encryption algorithm,
issuing CA, validity period, and public key value.
