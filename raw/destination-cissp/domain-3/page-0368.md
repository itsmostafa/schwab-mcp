Rail Fence (Zigzag)
Another simple form of transposition is called the Rail
Fence (zigzag) cipher and is illustrated in Figure 3-40.
Basically, the text is transposed by writing it in a table where
each row represents a rail, following a zigzag pattern.
Figure 3-40: Rail Fence Operation
Despite the seemingly more secure-looking output, this
form of transposition may still result in patterns being
recognizable.
