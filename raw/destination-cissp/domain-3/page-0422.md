Creating Digital Signatures
The process of creating a digital signature is quite easy and fundamentally
involves two steps as also shown in Figure 3-55: 1. The sender hashes the
message, which produces a fixed length message digest.
2. The sender encrypts the hash value with the sender’s private key.
Note that because a digital signature is basically just an encrypted hash value
with an output of 128/192/256/512 bits, digital signatures are very small in
size.
