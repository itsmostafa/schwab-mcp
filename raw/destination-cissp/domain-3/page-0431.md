Digital Certificate Replacement and Revocation
A best practice related to key management is periodic replacement or rotation
of keys, and the same applies to management of digital certificates. If a
public/private key pair is being replaced, this means that the accompanying
digital certificate would also need to be replaced. As part of normal business,
digital certificates are issued with an expiration date, and a given certificate
should be replaced on or before the expiry date in order to maintain
continuity of operations.
