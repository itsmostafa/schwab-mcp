 A single point of failure is something that, if failure is realized, will result
in negative operational impact.
 Redundancy can help alleviate the risk associated with a single point of
failure, and it should be implemented where it is cost-justifiable.
 Bypass controls are a potential vulnerability, and their existence creates
risks.
 The risks associated with bypass controls can be mitigated using
segregation of duties, logging and monitoring, and physical security.
 Time-of-Check Time-of-Use (TOCTOU), also known as a race condition,
represents a short window between two events, typically when
something is used and when authorization for that use is checked.
 Frequent access or authorization checks can reduce the risk of race
conditions.
 Emanations are unseen elements leaking out of systems that might
reveal confidential and valuable information if captured and analyzed
with the proper equipment.
 Shielding, white noise, and control zones can prevent emanations from
being captured.
