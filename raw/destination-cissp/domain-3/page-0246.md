Figure 3-3: Bell–LaPadula Model
This model is based on three basic principles:
1. Simple security property, also known as “no read up” property,
relates to reading and denotes that any subject at a particular
security level may not read an object at a higher security level.
2. The star (*) property, also known as “no write down” property,
relates to writing and denotes that any subject at a particular
security level may not write to an object at a lower security level.
