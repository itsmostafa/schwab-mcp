3.2.6 Evaluation Criteria (ITSEC and TCSEC)
CORE CONCEPTS
 TCSEC, also known as the Orange Book, is the first evaluation criteria
system.
 ITSEC followed TCSEC and was developed by Europeans to include
elements of the Orange Book and others.
 ITSEC applies to networked environments and measures functional and
assurance elements separate from one another.
