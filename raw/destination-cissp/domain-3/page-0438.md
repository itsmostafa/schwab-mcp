To obtain a digital certificate that contains her public key, Alice must utilize a
certificate authority (CA). A Registration Authority (RA) will ask Alice for
some identifying information as well as a copy of her public key and it will
“identity proof” Alice to confirm she is, in fact, Alice. After this step, Alice’s
public key will be signed by the root CA’s private key. The root CA is known
as the root of trust, but the signing process is usually facilitated through an
intermediary known as the intermediate or issuing CA. Once signed, the
intermediate/issuing CA issues the certificate.
