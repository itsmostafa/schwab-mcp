How Can We Be Certain We Have Someone’s Public Key?
What does a digital certificate contain?
To confirm with certainty someone’s public key, a copy of their digital
certificate is needed. Digital certificates bind individuals to their public keys.
The issuing Certificate Authority (CA) signs an individual certificate with
the CA’s private key, thereby ensuring the integrity and validity of the
certificate and public key being issued.
