An example of a storage covert channel exists in most technology
architectures. On a laptop, sensitive information could be placed in RAM,
because a process needs to use it, but when that process finishes, the sensitive
information remains present in memory. That could become available to other
processes that are placed in memory and can read it. The sensitive
information is not meant to be in RAM, but it is in fact there, and it could
disclose confidential information. That is an example of a storage covert
channel, which represents the majority of covert channels (roughly 99
percent).
