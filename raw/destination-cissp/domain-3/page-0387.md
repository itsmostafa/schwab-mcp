Understand advantages and disadvantages of
symmetric cryptography
Symmetric key cryptography is extremely fast and can
encrypt massive amounts of data. In the context of
networks—trusted and untrusted—where significant
amounts of data need to be encrypted/decrypted quickly,
symmetric cryptography tends to be the best solution.
However, key distribution is a glaring and inherent
weakness, especially if the parties involved in
communication are separated by any amount of distance.
Out-of-band communication can be used to overcome
this weakness, but this itself is not necessarily the best or
most feasible solution. Out-of-band implies that normal
communication channels are not utilized to exchange a key,
so internet-based solutions would not qualify. Rather, a
meeting in person, a voice call, or perhaps an SMS message
could be utilized for the purpose of key exchange. In
addition to the key distribution problem associated with
symmetric cryptography, a problem with scalability also
exists. Scalability refers to the number of symmetric keys
