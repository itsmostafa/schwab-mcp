3.1.2 Determining Appropriate Security Controls
CORE CONCEPTS
 Regardless of the framework, model, or methodology used, the risk
management process should be used to identify the most valuable
assets and risks to those assets, and to determine appropriate and
cost-effective security controls to implement this.
The design and creation of a secure system is not
constrained by one framework, model, or methodology. In
fact, many security concepts and principles can be applied
to its design. A number of secure design principles are
noted below, while some of them have been discussed
elsewhere in the book (as also referenced).
