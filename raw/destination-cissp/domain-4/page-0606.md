Bastion Host
Understand what is meant by the term “bastion host” and
where a bastion host might typically be found in a
network architecture
This risk can be mitigated through the creation of a
subnetwork, usually referred to as a Demilitarized Zone (DMZ),
where services and applications that require public access can
be segregated. The DMZ is not part of the internal network nor
is it part of the internet, it sits in between the two and it can be
controlled by the organization. As alluded to above, any service
or application that requires access from the outside—like web
applications, email, DNS, and remote access—can be placed in
the DMZ. Because the organization controls the DMZ, it can
also provide necessary protection for each application. In this
context, devices and applications within a DMZ are often
referred to as bastion hosts and bastion applications. The word
bastion is French and loosely translates to “fortress,” readied
