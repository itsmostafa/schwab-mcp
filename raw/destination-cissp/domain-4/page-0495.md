Although a lot of people simply refer to every type of
information as “packets,” that is actually incorrect. Information
within the uppermost three OSI layers (Application,
Presentation, and Session) is referred to as “data.” When that
reaches the Transport layer it is referred to as “segments” or
“datagrams.” At Layer 3 (Network), the term “packets” is used.
Layer 2 (Data Link) uses the term “frames” while at Layer 1
(Physical) everything is just referred to as bits (or 0s and 1s).
