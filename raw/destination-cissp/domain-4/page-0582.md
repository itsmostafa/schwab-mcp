Figure 4-22: ARP Poisoning
Monitoring of activities across network segments is one of the
best ways to prevent ARP poisoning. This holds true with
regards to DNS too, though in recent years, DNSSEC has been
developed and employed by many organizations to prevent
DNS poisoning. With all these vulnerabilities, if good preventive
or detective controls don’t exist, good compensating controls
should be implemented. This typically means more logging
and monitoring, depending on the value of the asset in
question.
