Topologies can be either physical or logical, with physical
topologies dictating how devices are physically linked and
how they communicate over these physical connections.
Logical topologies define how data actually flows within the
network, regardless of the physical layout. The important
thing to note is that even though a network may physically
have a star topology, logically, it could actually function as a
bus, with all communications being broadcast to all nodes.
The data plane is the doer. It’s what does the work of
transferring packets across the network based upon directions
from the control plane. The control plane is the intelligence
that tells the data plane what to do. Above this is the
management plane, which manages and monitors the
network’s operations.
