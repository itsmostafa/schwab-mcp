Virtual Private Network (VPN)
Know the most reliable and cost-effective way to securely
connect two networks
VPNs are one of the most reliable and cost-effective ways to
securely connect two networks together.
To create a VPN, tunneling plus encryption is needed. A
number of solutions exist that utilize SSL/TLS or IPsec VPN
technologies. Layer 2 and Layer 3 VPN solutions typically
provide both the functionality and efficiency required to host a
VPN.
