Routers
Devices that connect and route network traffic between
networks, based upon IP information included in received data
packets. Routers utilize routing protocols and typically
dynamically maintain routing tables to determine the optimal
route for data packet forwarding.
Switches
(Layer 3
switches)
Layer 3 switches function very similarly to routers and are often
used to connect devices on the same virtual local area network
(VLAN).
