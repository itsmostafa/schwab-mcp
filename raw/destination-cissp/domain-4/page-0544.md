TCP and UDP
As previously mentioned, Layer 4 (the Transport layer) is like a
truck that transports information between devices. Delivery
services can come in the form of reliable, ordered transmission
(using TCP) or in unreliable, unordered transmission (using
UDP). Many people refer to UDP as a “send and pray” protocol.
This said, both protocols are still heavily relied upon, and each
serves a purpose. For reliable, perhaps a bit slower,
transmissions, TCP is the clear choice. However, UDP is fast, and
for things like video streaming, which requires speed, as well as
handling DNS requests, UDP is very efficient.
