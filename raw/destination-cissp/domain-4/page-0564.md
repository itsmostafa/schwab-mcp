Session Initiation Protocol (SIP)
This is the secure version of RTP, which
supports encryption, authentication,
integrity, and replay attack protection.
Note that RTP is mainly used for
streaming voice and video over IP, with
no existing security in it. SRTP also
provides good bandwidth optimization,
low resource requirements, and is
independent from underlying
protocols. The full description of its
operation is described in RFC 3711.
SIP is responsible for initiating,
maintaining, and terminating voice and
video sessions. It can also support a
direct connection between PBX and
public telephony networks.
