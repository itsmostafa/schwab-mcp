Three-Legged Firewall
Figure 4-38 depicts a three-legged firewall (or three-zoned
firewall), by virtue of the three connection points, although any
number of connection points could really exist. This is
dependent on the needs and creativity of the organization. Any
number of firewall technologies can be utilized in the context
of this type of firewall, and the choices made in this regard
should directly reflect the security needs of the organization
and the assets being protected.
