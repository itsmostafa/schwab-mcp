The Transport layer is responsible for transporting information
that is being exchanged between devices. Think of it as the
truck that carries information between two people. To provide
reliable transportation, a road should be built that facilitates
ordered flow of traffic, including consistent reporting of road
conditions. Within the family of protocols mentioned above,
TCP is the protocol that provides ordered and reliable transport
service.
Conversely, unordered, and unreliable transport services can
also be provided by simply pointing the trucks toward the
destination and not relying on a specific road. In this case, the
trucks are simply loaded, and the drivers are told to go find the
destination. The drivers will likely follow different routes, arrive
in different order, and some may not arrive at all. All the trucks
may travel quickly, but otherwise they’ll be completely
unreliable. Within the family of protocols, UDP is the protocol
that operates in a similar manner—unordered and unreliable,
but very quick, transport.
