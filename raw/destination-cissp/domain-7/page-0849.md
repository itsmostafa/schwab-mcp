Both examples point to the real power of SIEM systems.
With traditional logging, where log files are only captured
and maintained on individual systems, it would take
enormous effort to access and analyze all the captured data
and determine if anything out of the ordinary or malicious is
taking place. It’s like trying to find the proverbial needle in
the haystack. With a SIEM system, significant intelligence is
incorporated into their functionality, which allows
significant amounts of logged events and analysis and
correlation of the same to take place very quickly. And with
the proper tuning, SIEM systems can identify and alert on
even the least apparent telltale sign.
