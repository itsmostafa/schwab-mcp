7.1.6 Five Rules of Evidence
CORE CONCEPTS
 The five rules of evidence state that evidence should be: authentic,
accurate, complete, convincing or reliable, and admissible.
 To best ensure that the five rules are achieved, evidence chain of
custody must be maintained.
For any evidence to stand the best chance of surviving legal
and other scrutiny, it should exhibit five characteristics, also
known as the “five rules of evidence,” described in Table 7-3.
