provisioning, baselining, automation)
7.3.1 Asset Inventory
CORE CONCEPTS
 Provisioning relates to the deployment of assets—hardware, software,
devices, and so on—within an organization.
 Part of provisioning should include maintaining and updating a
related asset inventory database anytime an asset is added or
removed.
 Assets should be managed as part of an overall asset management
life cycle.
