Geographically Remote/Geographic Disparity
Another topic of relevance is the notion of a recovery site being geographically remote. In other words,
if an organization’s primary site is on the East Coast of North America, perhaps the recovery site should
be somewhere in the Midwest or West Coast of North America. The idea is that you don’t want
whatever may have brought a primary site down to also impact the recovery site.
