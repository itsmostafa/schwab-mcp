Threat Intelligence
The term “threat intelligence” is an umbrella term
encompassing threat research and analysis and emerging
threat trends. It is an important element of any
organization’s digital security strategy that equips security
professionals to proactively anticipate, recognize, and
respond to threats. Many SIEM solutions offer threat
intelligence subscriptions that add additional capabilities,
strength, and value to already robust systems. However,
actionable threat intelligence can also be gleaned from
documents like vendor trend reports, public sector team
reports (like US-CERT), related information sharing and
analysis centers (ISACs), and more.
