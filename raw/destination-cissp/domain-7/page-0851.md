 After data has been normalized, correlation seeks to
line up events and determine which of them alone
and in combination might be important and
indicative of problems.
 Secure storage is the concept where the SIEM
system keeps a copy of all logged events from each
device. The system is designed to store data for long
periods of time, and ideally those log files are read-
only, to prevent tampering or deletion.
 Analysis and reporting refer to the SIEM system rules
that have been put in place, looking at data related
to the same, and taking action when appropriate. As
noted earlier, event log data can be collected from
virtually every system within an organization.
However, SIEM systems should be used to capture
relevant logs, based on organizational risk, required
budget, and regulatory obligations.
