7.6 Conduct incident management
7.6.1 Incident Response Process
CORE CONCEPTS
 Incident response is the process used to detect and respond to
incidents.
 Event = observable occurrence of something.
 Incident = an adverse event.
 Not all events are incidents.
 Incident response process includes: preparation, detection, response,
mitigation, reporting, recovery, remediation, and lessons learned.
