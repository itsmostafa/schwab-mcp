Artifacts
Understand the importance and potential relevance of
artifacts to an investigation
Forensic artifacts are remnants of a breach or attempted
breach of a system or network, and they may or may not be
relevant to an investigation or response. They’re
breadcrumbs that can potentially lead back to an intruder
or at least identify their actions and the path they followed
while in the system or network. Artifacts can be found in
numerous places, including:  Computer systems  Web
browsers  Mobile devices  Hard drives  Flash drives
Examples of artifacts include IP addresses, hashes, file
name/type, registry keys (Windows), URLs, operating system
information, as well as logged information, like account
updates, profile changes, file changes, and so on, that point
