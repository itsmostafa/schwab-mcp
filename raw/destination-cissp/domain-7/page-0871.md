Figure 7-7: Incident Response Process
Preparation
Preparation is critical, as this will help anticipate all the steps to follow.
Preparation would include things like developing the IR process, assigning
IR team members, and everything related to what happens when an
incident is identified.
Detection
Pointing back to the distinction between an event and an incident, the goal
of detection is to identify an adverse event—an incident—and to begin
dealing with it.
Response (IR
Team)
After an incident has been identified, the IR Team should be activated.
Among the first steps taken by the IR Team will be an impact assessment
to determine how big of a deal is the incident, how long might the impact
be experienced, who else might need to be involved, and so on.
