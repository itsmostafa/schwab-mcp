2.4.1 Information Life Cycle
CORE CONCEPTS
 Information life cycle phases
 Data must be protected at each phase
Information Life Cycle
Data must be protected at each stage of its life cycle. The concept of the information life cycle is
founded on the principle that proper controls should be in place at the time of creation and throughout
the life cycle. Immediately upon creation, collection, or update, information should be assigned a
classification (by the owner), which then drives all other activities, such as storage, use, sharing,
archiving, and final disposal or destruction, to protect information throughout its life cycle. These
stages are also highlighted in Figure 2-3, while their definitions are contained in Table 2-4. Also note
