Remediation
Based upon assessments and testing, remediation steps for all identified
vulnerabilities should be documented.
Exception
handling
If an identified vulnerability will not be remediated, this should be
documented too, including the reason why that’s the case. For example,
perhaps remediation would cost too much relative to the value of the asset
at risk or the chance of the risk being realized. Regardless of the reason,
exceptions should be carefully considered and noted.
Ethical
disclosure
Some identified vulnerabilities might be new discoveries and point to
significant flaws and weaknesses in widely used software and hardware.
In this context and for the sake of other users, customers, and vendors, it’s
very important that newly discovered vulnerabilities be shared to the
extent necessary to protect anybody who may be exposed to the same
vulnerabilities.
