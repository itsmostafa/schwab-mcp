Understand the role of a security professional
As might make sense, based upon everything written to this
point, testing should include all relevant stakeholders.
However, the role of security professionals is to do three
things:  Identify risk  Advise testing processes to ensure
risks are appropriately evaluated  Provide advice and
support to stakeholders The security team should not
actually perform the testing alone; however, security should
advise, provide assurance, monitor, support, and evaluate
results.
