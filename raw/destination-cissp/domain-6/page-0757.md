Assessment, Testing, and Auditing Strategies
As noted earlier, testing strategies are used to provide
assurance and can be considered from three broad
contexts: internal, external, and third party. Each can be
used alone or in combination, based on the type/level of
assurance sought.
Internal assessment/testing/auditing (e.g., within
organization control) are conducted by someone internal
to the organization, in other words, by an employee.
External assessment/testing/auditing (e.g., outside
organization control) can be defined in two different ways,
and both should be noted.
On one hand, a company that uses a certain application, for
example, Microsoft Azure, might use a team of employees to
look at Azure’s hosting environment, which is external to the
company. So, a company essentially audits or examines the
environment of an external service provider.
