It is estimated that a modern operating system can contain
something like fifty million lines of code. That’s a massive
number. Is there a chance that maybe a mistake or two
exist in those fifty million lines of code? Yes, it’s pretty much
guaranteed that many mistakes, bugs, unknown exploits,
and vulnerabilities may reside in fifty million lines of code.
With each passing day, systems in use around the globe are
becoming ever more complex. The more complex, the more
opportunity and likelihood for errors to exist. Furthermore,
not only are systems becoming more complex, but our
dependence upon them is becoming more critical and
pervasive, like planes using avionics systems with millions of
lines of code. Most planes today can fly themselves, with
very little manual intervention. However, air crashes still
happen because of fatal errors. Obviously, these
architectures are incredibly important, and it’s imperative
