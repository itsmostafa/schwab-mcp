CVSS
(Common

System)
CVSS, known as Common Vulnerability Scoring System, is a
framework that uses common vulnerability metrics and
characteristics to provide an average score of how severe a
vulnerability is. It assigns a number between 0 and 10 to any
vulnerability; the higher the number, the more severe and
critical the vulnerability. When a company identifies a new
vulnerability, a scoring methodology will be followed to
determine a score and then reported for inclusion in the CVSS
database.
