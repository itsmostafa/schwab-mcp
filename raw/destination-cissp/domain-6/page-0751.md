6.1 Design and validate assessment, test, and audit
strategies
Purpose of Security Assessment and Testing
Domain 6 focuses on security assessment and testing of
architectures, assets, and systems. Information covered in
earlier domains underscored the importance of every
control to address security from the perspective of the two
pillars—functional and assurance—that should support
every control. Security assessment and testing specifically
focuses on the latter—providing assurance to stakeholders,
how security is contributing to goals and objectives, and
that the right level of security is built into any architecture
that provides value to the organization. In other words, the
purpose of security assessment and testing is to ensure
that security requirements/controls are defined, tested, and
operating effectively. Additionally, security assessment and
testing apply to the development of new applications and
systems as well as the ongoing operations, including end-
of-life, related to assets.
