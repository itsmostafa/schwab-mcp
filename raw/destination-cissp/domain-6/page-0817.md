6.5.3 Audit Roles and Responsibilities
CORE CONCEPTS
 Audit roles include: executive (senior) management, audit committee,
security officer, compliance manager, internal auditors, and external
auditors.
 Audit responsibilities vary based upon the audit role.
It’s important that executive (senior) management understand that the tone must be set from the top,
and this applies to assurance as well. Yes, auditors are doing the work, but executive management must
clearly articulate that assurance is important and that the process is supported and a priority for the
organization.
