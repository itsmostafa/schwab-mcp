Included with verification are three terms that deserve
further examination: completeness, correctness, and
consistency (also known as the 3 Cs).
 Completeness means ensuring all the use cases of an
application, based upon all defined requirements
related to functionality, have been covered.
 Correctness refers to each “use” case representing
what’s supposed to be built.
 Consistency speaks to functionality being specified
consistently in all areas.
These three words capture the essence and philosophy of
application and system development.
