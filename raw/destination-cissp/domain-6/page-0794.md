The more information that can be gained about a system,
the easier it is to protect it or, conversely, attack it. Banner
grabbing helps identify software and versions, while
fingerprinting looks a bit more specifically at the way a
packet is formed and similar attributes to identify the
unique characteristics of a system—similar to the ridges of a
person’s fingerprint that make it unique.
