Threat Modeling Methodologies
CORE CONCEPTS
 Threat modeling is used to systematically identify, enumerate, and
prioritize threats related to an asset
Purpose of threat modeling
In order to perform proper risk management, it is important
to identify the threats and vulnerabilities associated with
each asset. Threat modeling methodologies aid in
systematically identifying threats and their severity, which in
turn makes risk management more accurate and effective.
