These are the core pillars of security, and, even though
referred to as the goals of information security, this is a
narrow view of what security needs to focus on today. The
goals of the three pillars—Confidentiality, Integrity, and
Availability—need to be applied to information and
everything else (assets) that represents value to the
organization. In other words, security and the core pillars
should be referred to as the “goals of asset security” and not
just “the goals of information security.”
