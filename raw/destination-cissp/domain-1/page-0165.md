Tailgating
and
Piggybacking
Tailgating or piggybacking is the action of following a
person who is authorized to enter a restricted area through a
door and thus gaining unauthorized access. The difference is
that in tailgating the attacker possesses a badge that is fake
but looks real. In piggybacking, the attacker doesn’t have
any badge at all.
