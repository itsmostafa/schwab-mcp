The list above illustrates just a few of the privacy laws
around the world, and the requirements in these laws vary
from country to country. You are not expected to be a
privacy expert for purposes of the CISSP exam; but as a
security professional you should understand that privacy
cannot be achieved without security. Security must be
involved in implementing the required security controls to
achieve the required privacy requirements.
One privacy law that you should have a slightly deeper
understanding of is GDPR. The reason is that GDPR is
considered by many to be the bellwether for privacy laws in
countries around the world. GDPR is one of the most
comprehensive privacy laws in the world, and many
