Definition of awareness
Training provides specific skills needed to perform tasks
related to security. It often focuses on the technical aspects
of a role. Examples of training might include a firewall
administrator learning how to write firewall rules or a
security guard learning how to respond to different
situations related to protecting a building and the assets
within.
Finally, education helps people understand fundamental
concepts and therefore develop decision-making skills and
abilities. Table 1-33 contains a comparison between these
three.
