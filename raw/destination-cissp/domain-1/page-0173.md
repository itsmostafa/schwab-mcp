Service Level Reports
Service level reports are issued by a vendor or service
provider to a client and provide insight and information
about the service provider’s ability to deliver services as
defined by the SLA. The service level report compares
anticipated and agreed upon service levels with actual
service levels and documents the effectiveness of security
controls, which allows the customer—the owner—to gain
assurance that expectations are being met.
