The simplest definition of personal data is data that can be
used on its own or in combination to identify an individual.
Personal data can be referred to as:  PI—Personal
Information  PII—Personally Identifiable Information  SPI—
Sensitive Personal Information  PHI—Protected Health
Information How is personal data defined in a little more
