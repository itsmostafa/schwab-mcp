What do trade secrets, patents, copyrights, and
trademarks protect?
Intellectual property is any intangible product (invention,
formula, algorithm, literary work, song, symbol, etc.) of the
human intellect that the law protects from unauthorized
use by others. Intellectual property laws (outlined in Table 1-
7) help protect intellectual property assets with the goal of
encouraging the creation of a wide variety of intellectual
goods. Though intellectual property laws and regulations
vary quite a bit from country to country, the basic premises
remain the same, as noted in Table 1-7.
