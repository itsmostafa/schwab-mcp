Asset valuation
During the first step of risk management, all efforts are
focused on identifying the tangible and intangible assets
that are of greatest value to the organization. These assets
can vary widely: they can be buildings and equipment,
critical business processes, the reputation of the company,
and many others. Two different forms of analysis can be
used to rank the assets of the organization from most to
