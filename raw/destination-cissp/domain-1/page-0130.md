Risk Management Terms
Table 1-20 contains a list of core terms used in risk
management and how they fit together.
Threat Agent
Entity that has the potential to cause damage to an
asset (e.g., external attackers, internal attackers,
disgruntled employees)
Threat
Any potential danger
Attack
Any harmful action that exploits a vulnerability
Vulnerability
A weakness in an asset that could be exploited by a
threat
