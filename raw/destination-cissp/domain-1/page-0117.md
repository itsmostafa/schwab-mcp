Employee Duress
An employee acting under duress may be forced to perform
an action or set of actions that they wouldn’t do under
normal circumstances. For example, consider the scenario
of a bank manager threatened by an attacker and told to
open the bank’s vault while held at gunpoint. In that
scenario, the bank manager’s life is at risk, so, acting under
duress, they may give the attacker what they want. One
common practice to handle these stressful situations is to
have keywords that denote that an employee is acting
under duress. Have you ever watched The Bourne Identity?
You will notice that at some point one of the field agents
calls the CIA headquarters and gives the all clear after
inspecting a potentially compromised location. However,
the agent in charge does a challenge-response check and
expects a specific answer to be provided if the field agent is
