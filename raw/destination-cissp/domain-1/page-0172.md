Service Level Agreement (SLA)
One important note: even though the agreement is
between a service provider and a customer, the customer
remains accountable for all customer data being processed
by the provider. SLAs are addendums to the contract and
are therefore enforceable. SLAs often include expectations
and stipulations related to:  Service Levels (performance
levels)  Governance—the customer and the service provider
know who is responsible for what  Security—expected
security controls put in place by the service provider that
speak to the topic of accountability and responsibility.
Accountability can never be outsourced; thus, the security
controls needed to protect customer data must be very
clearly defined by the customer and put in place to exact
specifications by the service provider.
