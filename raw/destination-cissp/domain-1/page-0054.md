DOMAIN 1
SECURITY AND RISK MANAGEMENT
The first CISSP domain focuses on the fundamentals of
security and how to assess and manage risk. You will learn
the concepts of the CIA triad, gain insight into core
organizational roles and how they relate to security, and
understand the important difference between
accountability and responsibility, in addition to corporate
laws (policies) and key processes like risk analysis. This
domain also focuses heavily on the key factors of
governance and compliance, and how security helps by
being aligned and contributing to each.
