1.2.1 Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Authenticity,
and Nonrepudiation
CORE CONCEPTS
 Confidentiality: Protects and prevents unauthorized disclosure
 Integrity: Protects and adds value to assets by making them more
accurate, more timely, more current, more meaningful
 Availability: Ensures organizational assets are available when required
by stakeholders
 Authenticity: Proves the source and origin of important valuable
assets
 Nonrepudiation: Provides assurance that someone cannot deny
having done something
